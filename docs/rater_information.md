# Information for raters

**Project:** COMP8240 (Macquarie University) student project on sentence-similarity models.
**What you do:** rate how similar the meaning of about 60 sentence pairs is, on a 0-4 scale
(see `annotation_guidelines.md`). It takes roughly 30-45 minutes.
**Voluntary:** you can stop at any time and ask for your ratings to be deleted.
**Your data:** no personal information is collected. You are identified only as "rater 2", "rater 3".
Your ratings (numbers only) will be used to measure agreement and may be published in the project's
public repository and report, without your name.
**Payment:** none. **Risks:** none beyond everyday reading. **Questions:** contact the student.

By returning your ratings you confirm that you have read this information and agree to take part.
